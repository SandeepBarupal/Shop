Returns, Exchanges & Play Guarantee

Our goal is your complete satisfaction on the golf course. Learn how our on-spot shoe fitting and 30-day play guarantee protect your investment.

1. 7-Day Easy Returns on New Equipment

Brand-new golf apparel, footwear, rangefinders, GPS watches, and golf bags may be returned or exchanged within 7 days of delivery for a 100% full refund. New golf clubs must remain in original factory shrinkwrap with all tags and accessories intact.

2. On-the-Spot Try & Exchange (White-Glove Delivery)

During doorstep White-Glove delivery in Delhi NCR and Bengaluru, customers purchasing FootJoy golf shoes or tour apparel are encouraged to try them on while our fleet specialist waits. If a different size is needed, the driver can swap it on the spot or arrange a same-day replacement.

3. 30-Day Playability Guarantee (Certified Pre-Owned)

Inspired by Golf Garage's player-first policy, all Certified Pre-Owned golf clubs include our 30-Day Playability Guarantee. If you take a pre-owned driver, fairway wood, or iron set out to your course and feel the shaft stiffness or launch trajectory is not suitable for your swing, return it within 30 days for 100% store credit toward any other equipment.

4. Doorstep Pickup & Refund Processing

Returns in covered fleet cities are collected from your home or golf course locker for free by our uniformed drivers. Once verified at our studio hub, refunds are credited back to your original payment method (UPI / Card / Net Banking) within 48 to 72 business hours.

5. How to Initiate a Return

To initiate an exchange or return, visit your My Orders page or contact our WhatsApp Concierge at +91 98710 44321.
