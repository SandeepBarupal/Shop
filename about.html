THE CLUBHOUSE ETHOS

Born from the Spirit of the Game

Inspired by the timeless golf heritage of the Home of Golf in St Andrews and engineered with modern tour precision. Royal Fairway was founded to elevate golf retail and custom fitting across India.

OUR HERITAGE

A New Benchmark in Indian Golf Commerce

For too long, golfers in India faced fragmented supply chains, uncertainty around authenticity, and delayed deliveries. We built Royal Fairway Golf Co. with a single conviction: golfers deserve an uncompromising, international-standard experience from unboxing to the 18th green.

Every club in our collection is sourced directly from authorized manufacturers—Titleist, TaylorMade, Callaway, Ping, Mizuno, and Scotty Cameron. Each club is laser-etched with verified serial numbers and hand-delivered by our uniformed White-Glove delivery fleet.
