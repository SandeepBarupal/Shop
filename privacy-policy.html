LEGAL & COMPLIANCE

Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and the Information Technology Act 2000.

1. Information We Collect

Royal Fairway Golf Co. collects personal information essential for executing orders, booking custom TrackMan fittings, and dispatching White-Glove deliveries. This includes your name, delivery address, contact phone number, email address, handicap index, and biometric fitting measurements (e.g. swing speed, launch angle, wrist-to-floor distance).

2. Payment Information Security

All payment transactions (UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking) are encrypted using 256-bit SSL protocols and processed through RBI-regulated payment gateways. We never store credit card numbers, CVV codes, or net banking passwords on our servers.

3. Driver Communication & Live Tracking

When an order is dispatched via our dedicated White-Glove fleet, your delivery location and contact number are transmitted strictly to the authorized uniformed driver assigned to your route for gate coordination and identity verification.

4. No Sale of Customer Information

We hold a zero-tolerance policy against selling, renting, or trading player information to third-party telemarketers or advertisers.

5. Data Retention & Player Rights

In accordance with the DPDP Act 2023, you have the right to request a full copy of your player profile, fitting history, or request permanent deletion of your account records by emailing privacy@royalfairway.com.
