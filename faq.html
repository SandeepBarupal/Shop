🛡 Equipment Authenticity & Warranty

Are all golf clubs and equipment 100% genuine and authentic? +
Yes, absolutely. Royal Fairway Golf Co. is an authorized retail distributor for Titleist, TaylorMade, Callaway, Ping, Mizuno, FootJoy, Bushnell, and Scotty Cameron. Every club features verifiable manufacturer laser-etched serial numbers that can be registered directly with the brand for warranty.
What manufacturer warranty is included with new golf clubs? +
All brand-new golf equipment includes the full official 2-year manufacturer warranty covering head structural integrity, carbon composite face delamination, and shaft defects. Royal Fairway facilitates all warranty claims locally through our Gurugram and Bengaluru studios.
How can I verify the serial number on my club? +
Each wood and iron hosel contains a micro-engraved serial number. Upon delivery, your physical receipt and digital certificate list this exact serial code, verified by our master clubmaker prior to dispatch.

🚐 White-Glove Delivery Fleet

What makes Royal Fairway White-Glove Delivery unique? +
Unlike ordinary third-party couriers who stack golf bags roughly in cargo trucks, our deliveries are carried in dedicated climate-controlled Fairway EV Vans by uniformed golf technicians. Your equipment remains upright, cushioned in velvet travel cases, and is inspected with you upon arrival.
Which cities are covered by the dedicated White-Glove fleet? +
We operate our in-house fleet across all of Delhi NCR (Gurugram, New Delhi, Noida, Greater Noida, Faridabad) and Bengaluru (Indiranagar, Whitefield, Koramangala, Sadashivanagar). For other cities including Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad, Chandigarh, and Kolkata, orders are dispatched via expedited insured air freight.
Can you deliver directly to my golf course locker or pro shop? +
Yes! Simply select "Golf Club Locker" at checkout and indicate your club (e.g. DLF Golf & Country Club, Delhi Golf Club, KGA, or Prestige Golfshire) and locker number or caddie master desk. Our fleet specialists have verified access credentials.

🔄 Certified Pre-Owned & Trade-In

How does the 10-Point Pre-Owned condition grading work? +
Inspired by Golf Garage's benchmark standards, every pre-owned club is rated from 10/10 Mint (Unhit/Like New) down to 7/10 Good. We inspect crown condition, face grooves, sole wear, shaft frequency/straightness, grip tackiness, and headcover authenticity.
How do I trade-in my old clubs for store credit? +
Visit our Trade-In Valuation Tool, choose your club brand, model, and condition. You will receive an instant rupee quote. Once approved, our White-Glove fleet will pick up the clubs from your doorstep for free, and issue store credit instantly.
What is the 30-Day Playability Guarantee? +
If your certified pre-owned driver or iron set does not fit your swing on the golf course, you may exchange it for 100% store credit toward any other equipment within 30 days of delivery.

💳 Returns & Payments

What payment methods are supported? +
We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), RuPay, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Net Banking across all top Indian banks, and Cash/UPI on Delivery (COD) via our fleet couriers.
What is the return and exchange timeline? +
We offer a 7-day hassle-free return policy on brand-new apparel, shoes, and unhit clubs in original shrinkwrap. You can also try shoes for size during your White-Glove delivery and hand them back immediately if an alternate size is preferred.
