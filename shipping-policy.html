White-Glove Fleet Shipping Policy

How we safely transport championship golf clubs, bespoke shafts, and luxury golf leather directly to your door or golf course.

1. Dedicated White-Glove Fleet Network

Royal Fairway operates an exclusive in-house fleet of climate-controlled Electric Delivery Vans (EV) staffed by uniformed golf equipment technicians. This ensures delicate carbon-fiber driver heads, milled putters, and graphite shafts never undergo rough conveyor sorting or heat exposure during transit.

2. Geographic Coverage & Delivery Windows

  • • Delhi NCR (Gurugram, New Delhi, Noida, Faridabad): Same-Day / Next-Day White-Glove Fleet delivery. Time slots: 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM and 3:00 PM – 7:00 PM.
  • • Bengaluru Metro (Indiranagar, Whitefield, Koramangala, Sadashivanagar): Next-Day White-Glove Fleet delivery dispatched from KGA Hub.
  • • Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Chandigarh, Kolkata: 2 to 3 Business Days via Priority Insured Air Courier (BlueDart Apex).

3. Free Delivery Threshold & Charges

All orders of ₹4,999 or greater qualify automatically for Free White-Glove Own-Delivery. For orders under ₹4,999 (e.g. single glove or accessory pouch), a nominal nominal delivery fee of ₹250 is applied at checkout.

4. Golf Club Locker & Caddie Master Delivery

Golfers playing morning rounds can request direct delivery to their club pro shop or locker room at prominent courses including DLF Golf & Country Club, Delhi Golf Club, Jaypee Greens, Classic Golf Resort, Karnataka Golf Association, and Prestige Golfshire. Our drivers have vetted security credentials.

5. Full Transit Insurance

Every package is 100% insured against loss, transit impact, or weather damage from our studio dispatch door to your hands. If any damage occurs during transit, a brand-new replacement is immediately released.

6. Driver Coordination & Tracking

Once an order departs our fitting hub, the assigned driver's full name, mobile number, vehicle number plate, and live route status become accessible on your Live Order Tracking console.
